🐉 Jackie Chan's Adventures

Jackie! The Dark Hand gang has recently started targeting us. Walter is in need of all the 8 Banishment Objects of the demons now. You very well know the effect of losing those banishment objects. If it happens, then Shendu and all the other demons will get liberty. That is dangerous for the entire world. To prevent that, you should go and search all the lost banishment objects and bring them back to Section-13. Only then, they all could be prevented from reaching the hands of the Dark Hand. All the way you go, Shadow Guards will follow you to steal the banishment objects from you. Your job is to protect them all from the Shadow Guards. At this situation, I can trust only you. I have hope in you. Safeguard them all from going to the hands of Dark Hand. All the way you go, the 12 talismans will protect you from the shadow guards. All the best!

📜 How to Play

🏃 Movement

Choose Arrows or WASD in the Controls box on the left. Left/Right (A/D) move, Up (W) jumps or flies up with Rooster, Down (S) flies down.

⚔️ Attacking Enemies

Defeat 🥷 Shadow Guards (10 pts) and 🦇 Bats (15 pts). Press Q for the Pufferfish Chi Laser or E for the Lizard Magic Laser (both fire straight in the direction you face). With the Dragon talisman, click to shoot fireballs at your mouse. With the Pig talisman, an eye laser follows your mouse and destroys enemies it touches.

🐾 Talismans

Press 1–0, - or click the buttons at the bottom. Press again to turn one off. Several can be active at once, and each has a timer (except Dragon and Ox, which stay on until you turn them off).

🐕 Invulnerable · 🐉 Fireballs · 🐴 Heals +20 HP · 🐒 Shape Transform to Ninja · 🐀 Super Moose · 🐇 Super Speed · 🐷 Eye Laser · 🐂 Giant (Half Damage) · 🐓 Flight · 🐑 Astral Spirit · 🐍 Invisibility · 🦎/🐡 One Shot Magic Laser

🗡️ Banishment Objects

Find the demons' Banishment Objects and collect them: 🗡️ Shendu · 🪭 Xiao Fung · 🍐 Bai Tza · 🌸 Dai Gui · 🪷 Tso Lan · 🪈 Hsi Wu · 🥟 Tchang Zu · 🪘 Po Kong. Check the board under your HP bar: 🟡 indicates not collected yet, 🟢 indicates collected. Once collected, no object can be lost because of shadow guards or bats.

🎭 Oni Masks

Find all the 10 Oni Masks of ancient war heads and collect them.

🐅 2-Player Co-Op

Press = (Tiger) to open an online session and share the Session ID, or paste a friend's Session ID in the Online Co-Op box to join. Everyone plays their own blue Jackie and sees the other player as the red Jackie. Press = again to return to single player.

🌊 Water

Avoid the water gaps in the ground. Standing in water drains your HP continuously. Jump or fly over them, or use the Dog talisman to stay safe.

⏸️ Pause

Press P or Esc, or click the Pause button at the bottom right, to pause or resume.

❤️ Survive

Dodge the silver blades and avoid touching enemies. The game ends when your HP reaches 0. The longer you survive, the faster enemies spawn!
