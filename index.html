🐉 Jackie Chan's Adventures

Jackie! The Dark Hand gang has recently started targeting us. Walter is in need of all the 12 talismans now. You very well know what will happen if those talismans fall into his hands. If it happens, then Shendu will get liberty and that is dangerous for the entire world. To prevent that, the talismans should not go to the hands of the Dark Hand. All the way you go, Shadow Guards will follow you to steal the talismans from you. Protecting the talismans from the Shadow Guards sent by Walter is your job. I have hope in you and at this situation, I can trust only you. Safeguard the talismans from going to the hands of Dark Hand. All the best!

📜 How to Play

🏃 Movement

Choose Arrows or WASD in the Controls box on the left. Left/Right (A/D) move, Up (W) jumps or flies up with Rooster, Down (S) flies down.

⚔️ Attacking Enemies

Defeat 🥷 Shadow Guards (100 pts) and 🦇 Bats (150 pts). Press Q for the Pufferfish Chi Laser (fires straight in the direction you face). With the Dragon talisman, click to shoot fireballs at your mouse. With the Pig talisman, an eye laser follows your mouse and destroys enemies it touches.

🐾 Talismans

Press 1–0, - or click the buttons at the bottom. Press again to turn one off. Several can be active at once, and each has a timer (except Dragon and Ox, which stay on until you turn them off).

🐕 Invulnerable · 🐉 Fireballs · 🐴 Heal +20 HP · 🐒 Shape Transform to Ninja · 🐀 Giving Movement for Trees · 🐇 Super Speed · 🐷 Eye Laser · 🐂 Giant (Half Damage) · 🐓 Flight · 🐑 Astral Spirit · 🐍 Invisibility · 🦎/🐡 One Shot Magic Laser

🐅 2-Player Co-Op

Press = (Tiger) to open an online session and share the Session ID, or paste a friend's Session ID in the Online Co-Op box to join. Everyone plays their own blue Jackie and sees the other player as the red Jackie. Press = again to return to single player.

❤️ Survive

Dodge arrows and avoid touching enemies. The game ends when your HP reaches 0. The longer you survive, the faster enemies spawn!
